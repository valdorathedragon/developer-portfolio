## Binary Number Representation

### Decimal 75 to Binary (Division by 2 Method)
* 75 ÷ 2 = 37 remainder 1 (LSB)
* 37 ÷ 2 = 18 remainder 1
* 18 ÷ 2 = 9 remainder 0
* 9 ÷ 2 = 4 remainder 1
* 4 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 0
* 2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0
* 1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1 (MSB)

Reading the remainders from bottom to top: **75 (decimal) = 1001011 (binary)**

### Binary 110101 to Decimal (Positional Weight Method)
* 1 × 2⁵ = 1 × 32 = 32
* 1 × 2⁴ = 1 × 16 = 16
* 0 × 2³ = 0 × 8 = 0
* 1 × 2² = 1 × 4 = 4
* 0 × 2¹ = 0 × 2 = 0
* 1 × 2⁰ = 1 × 1 = 1

**Sum:** 32 + 16 + 0 + 4 + 0 + 1 = **53 (decimal)**

### Why Computers Use Binary Internally
Computers use binary internally because their physical transistors rely on electricity with only two reliable states: OFF (0) and ON (1).
